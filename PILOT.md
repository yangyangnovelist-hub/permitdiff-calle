# Join the PermitDiff pilot

PermitDiff separates two kinds of validation so a synthetic phone demo is never presented as
customer adoption.

## Track A — consented transport validation

Any adult in a CALL-E-supported calling region may volunteer for one short, AI-disclosed role-play
using the fixed Example City permit fixture in [`LIVE-VALIDATION.md`](LIVE-VALIDATION.md). The
recipient receives the script in advance. This proves real CALL-E transport and discrepancy
routing only; it does not represent a government office or real permit outcome.

## Track B — contractor workflow pilot

An authorized applicant, contractor permit coordinator or expeditor can test the no-call gate and
evidence review workflow against a real record they are allowed to access. PermitDiff will not call
an actual office unless that exact recipient has separately authorized the AI call. The pilot
measures calls avoided, eligible exceptions, answered outcomes, discrepancies surfaced, operator
minutes and duplicate-call rate—not approval speed.

## Privacy and consent

No random business, government office or uninvolved third party is called. A participant may stop
at any time. Public proof excludes phone numbers, identities, transcripts, recordings, addresses
and real permit identifiers; any real-case write-up requires separate approval.

To volunteer, use **Message manager** on the
[PermitDiff Devpost page](https://devpost.com/software/permitdiff). Do not post a phone number in a
public issue or discussion. The exact scenario and publication boundary will be shared first.
